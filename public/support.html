Common problems

An upload says it failed

Open the recording in the Archive and read the error. If it mentions transcription, the file may be silent or in an unusual format: convert it to mp3 or m4a and try again. On the hosted version files must be under 4 MB.

The answer says they never talked about it

That is the archive being honest. Search found nothing that answers the question. Try different words (a dish name, a person, a place), or save the suggested question and ask it on the next call.

An answer showed "Removed 1 quote"

Gemma wrote a quote that is not in any transcript, so Legacy Loom took that sentence out. The rest of the answer is still grounded in the recordings. Nothing for you to fix.

The call planner will not show a forecast

It needs at least 8 rated recordings, with some rated 4 or 5 and some lower. Rate recordings in the Archive, or import a call log CSV on the Next call page.

I cannot add or rate anything

The archive is read only without the passcode. Enter it in Settings in the app. It is saved on that device only.

Running locally: Gemma shows as offline

Ollama is not running. Start the Ollama app (or run ollama serve) and check that gemma3:4b appears in ollama list.

Running locally: Atlas will not connect

In Atlas, open Security, then Network Access, and add your IP address or 0.0.0.0/0. An SSL handshake error almost always means the IP is not allowed yet.
