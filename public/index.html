How it works

From a voice note to an answer, in three steps

  1. Listen

    Whisper writes down every word with its timestamp, on your own laptop. Online, ElevenLabs Scribe does the same.

  2. File

    Gemma 4 reads the transcript and pulls out the recipe, the people, the year and the lines worth keeping word for word.

  3. Ask

    Answers come only from the recordings. Tap a citation and you hear the exact second it was said.
